---
Estimation Theory
---

Consistency in terms of **Mean Squared Error (MSE)** means:

```math
\lim_{n \to \infty} \text{MSE}(\hat{\theta}_n) = 0
```

Since:

```math
\text{MSE}(\hat{\theta}_n) = \operatorname{Var}(\hat{\theta}_n) + \left[\operatorname{Bias}(\hat{\theta}_n)\right]^2
```

Both variance and bias must vanish asymptotically:

- $\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\theta}_n) \to 0$
- $\operatorname{Bias}(\hat{\theta}_n) \to 0$

Thus, MSE → 0 ⇒ estimator is consistent.
